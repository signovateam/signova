Be understood, right away

Most people don't know sign language, and an interpreter is rarely there when you need one. Signova speaks for you, so everyday moments get easier.

  • Speaks instantly

    Make a sign and the glove says it out loud. No typing, no waiting.

  • Works anywhere

    No phone and no internet needed, so it works in a hospital, a shop or a taxi.

  • Comfortable to wear

    A soft stretch glove with a small unit on the wrist. Nothing to hold.

  • Easy to power

    Runs from an ordinary USB power bank. Charge it like a phone.

Three steps. That's it.

No setup apps, no accounts.

  1. Put it on

    Switch it on, then hold your hand flat and make a fist for 3 seconds so it learns your hand.

  2. Sign

    Make one of the 24 signs it knows and hold it steady for a moment.

  3. It speaks

    The glove says the phrase out loud, in clear English.
